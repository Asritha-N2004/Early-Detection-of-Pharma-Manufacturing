# GMP and data integrity design considerations

This prototype is not certified, validated, qualified, or inherently compliant. It does not provide a Part 11 electronic signature. It provides evidence to authorized reviewers; it cannot confirm a GMP deviation, release a batch or authorize manufacturing.

ALCOA+ design mapping: attribution comes from authenticated identities and execution actors; legibility from structured reports; timestamps use UTC; original measurement payloads and rejected quality events are preserved; accuracy relies on syntactic/registry checks and still needs calibrated instruments; completeness and consistency require reconciliation, batch-stage context and source verification beyond this prototype; enduring/available records require qualified backups, restore procedures, retention and archival controls. Audit records contain a SHA-256 predecessor chain with mutation triggers. Privileged owners can bypass these controls; external immutable anchoring and segregated audit administration remain required.

Before regulated use, quality personnel must determine applicable 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11/15, ICH Q8/Q9/Q10 and GAMP 5 obligations using current authoritative texts. This document is an engineering gap inventory, not legal advice or an assertion of compliance. Required work includes intended-use specification, computerized-system risk assessment, supplier assessment, infrastructure qualification, IQ/OQ/PQ, approved SOPs, validated source integration, access reviews, electronic-signature binding and identity verification, audit review, controlled changes, model qualification, incident handling, disaster recovery and periodic review. No regulatory interpretations are implemented as automatic approval rules.

Demonstration operating ranges and FMEA thresholds have no quality approval. Trained models remain unapproved; controlled-mode inference fails closed. SOP-approved criticality rules must supersede simplistic RPN cutoffs. Reports identify missing equipment/operator/source evidence and root causes as hypotheses. Ensure data retention and audit access remain available even when models or configurations are superseded.
